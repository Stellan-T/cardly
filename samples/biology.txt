# Biology: cells and energy

Photosynthesis: process by which plants make glucose using light
Cellular respiration: process that releases energy from glucose, producing ATP, water and carbon dioxide
Mitochondria: organelle where cellular respiration takes place; the "powerhouse" of the cell
Chloroplast: organelle in plant cells where photosynthesis takes place
Nucleus: membrane-bound organelle that contains the cell's DNA
Ribosome: structure that builds proteins from amino acids
Cell membrane: thin, flexible barrier that controls what enters and leaves the cell
Cell wall: rigid outer layer of plant, fungal and bacterial cells
Cytoplasm: jelly-like fluid that fills the cell and holds the organelles
Vacuole: sac that stores water, food and waste
Endoplasmic reticulum: network of membranes that transports proteins and lipids
Golgi apparatus: organelle that modifies, sorts and packages proteins
Lysosome: organelle containing enzymes that break down waste
ATP: adenosine triphosphate, the main energy carrier of the cell
Enzyme: protein that speeds up a chemical reaction
Diffusion: movement of particles from high to low concentration
Osmosis: diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane
Active transport: movement of substances against a concentration gradient, using energy
Mitosis: cell division that produces two identical daughter cells
Meiosis: cell division that produces four gametes with half the chromosome number
Chromosome: thread-like structure of DNA and protein that carries genes
Gene: section of DNA that codes for a protein
DNA: deoxyribonucleic acid, the molecule that carries genetic information
RNA: ribonucleic acid, which helps turn genetic information into proteins
Prokaryote: single-celled organism without a nucleus
Eukaryote: organism whose cells have a nucleus
Chlorophyll: green pigment that absorbs light for photosynthesis
Glucose: simple sugar made in photosynthesis (C6H12O6)
Homeostasis: keeping a stable internal environment
Stomata: small openings in leaves that let gases in and out
Fermentation: releasing energy from glucose without oxygen
Organelle: specialised structure inside a cell with a specific job
